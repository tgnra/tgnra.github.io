---
title: Notes on slow photography
summary: Taking fewer pictures, on purpose, and what that did to the ones I kept.
---

For a month I limited myself to twelve frames a day. It sounds like a gimmick, and it partly is, but it changed how I walk.

## What changed

I stopped shooting things I'd never look at again. I started waiting for the light to do something. Most days I didn't even use all twelve.

## What didn't

I still delete about half of what I take. The keepers are just better.
